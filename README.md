# ParcelResolve

**ParcelResolve** is an automated parcel incident resolution platform designed for large e-commerce and delivery organizations.

The system focuses on what happens **when something goes wrong with a parcel** — rather than replacing the organization's existing ordering, tracking, warehouse, or delivery systems.

## The Problem

Large-scale parcel operations generate many delivery incidents, including:

- Lost parcels
- Delayed parcels
- Misdelivered parcels
- Failed deliveries
- Damaged parcels
- Delivery disputes
- Other delivery exceptions

Resolving these incidents can require information and actions from multiple teams. Existing operational systems may contain the necessary information, but the resolution process can still depend heavily on manual investigation, communication, and follow-up.

This can result in:

- Slow resolution
- Repeated manual work
- Unclear responsibility
- Missed follow-ups
- Inconsistent handling of similar incidents
- Poor visibility into incident progress
- Poor customer experience

## The Idea

ParcelResolve acts as an **incident resolution layer** on top of the organization's existing operational systems.

Instead of simply recording a problem, the system should help move the incident toward a resolution.

The initial concept is:

```text
Detect
  ↓
Classify
  ↓
Analyze
  ↓
Determine next action
  ↓
Assign responsibility
  ↓
Execute and monitor
  ↓
Escalate when necessary
  ↓
Resolve
```

The system should automate routine resolution workflows while allowing human intervention when a situation requires judgement or falls outside predefined rules.

## What ParcelResolve Is Not

ParcelResolve is not intended to replace:

- Order management systems
- Parcel tracking systems
- Warehouse management systems
- Route optimization systems
- Driver navigation
- Physical delivery operations
- Customer service teams

Instead, it connects relevant operational information and processes to manage **delivery incidents and their resolution**.

## Project Direction

The project is currently in the **concept and requirements exploration stage**.

The system will be progressively developed through:

- Product and problem analysis
- Stakeholder analysis
- Requirements engineering
- Workflow modelling
- Business rules
- System design
- Architecture
- User experience
- Implementation

The exact functionality and system boundaries will be refined as the project develops.
